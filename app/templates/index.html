✔ 100% In-Memory Processing · Zero User Profiling

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Metavoid is designed as an offline-first digital hygiene tool. We believe the safest data is data that is never stored.

1. Data Processing Architecture

  • In-Memory Scrubbing: Image processing takes place entirely in volatile RAM. Files are scrubbed and streamed directly back to you. They are never written to permanent disk storage.
  • Zero Training Retention: We never store your images, and we never use your images, metadata, or generation prompts to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Desktop Application: When running the standalone desktop version, processing occurs strictly on your local PC (127.0.0.1). Exactly zero bytes of image data leave your machine.
  • Hosted Web Application: Uploaded files exist in memory only for the duration of the HTTP request and are purged immediately. Temporary server access logs are retained for a maximum of 48 hours solely for DDoS protection before being permanently overwritten.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, tracking cookies, browser fingerprinting data, or extracted GPS/biometric data.

3. GDPR (EEA/UK) & CCPA/CPRA (California) Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, and port your data, as well as the right to non-discrimination. Because we do not create accounts or store files, no personal data is retained to sell or share. You can click "Clear session data" in the app at any time to instantly flush all cached thumbnails and in-memory references.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Metavoid is a general utility tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not collect or maintain information from children.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries: contact@metavoid.app.
