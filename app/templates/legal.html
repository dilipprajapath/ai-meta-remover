Privacy Policy

Last revised: September 2026

Metavoid is designed from the ground up as a digital hygiene tool. We believe the safest data is data we never store. This policy outlines exactly what happens when you process an image.

1. Data Processing Architecture

  • In-Memory Scrubbing: When you submit files for metadata stripping, processing takes place entirely in volatile RAM. Files are cleaned and streamed directly back to you. They are never written to permanent disk storage.
  • Zero Training Retention: We never store your images, and we never use your images, metadata, or prompts to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Local Desktop Execution: When using the standalone Windows desktop application, all processing happens strictly on your local device (127.0.0.1). Exactly zero bytes of file data or metadata leave your computer.
  • Hosted Web Execution: When accessing our web deployment, uploaded files remain in memory only for the duration of the HTTP request and are pruned immediately. Temporary server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) are retained for a maximum of 48 hours solely for DDoS mitigation and infrastructure security before being permanently overwritten.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Payment information or credit card numbers.
  • Tracking cookies, browser fingerprinting profiles, or behavioral advertising data.
  • GPS coordinates, camera metadata, or biometric data extracted from your images.

3. Rights Under GDPR (EEA/UK) & CCPA/CPRA (California)

Under international data protection regulations (including the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act), you have the right to access, rectify, port, and delete personal information, as well as the right to non-discrimination. Because we do not collect personal profiles or retain uploaded images, we hold no user data to sell or share. You may click the "Clear Session Data" button in the interface at any time to instantly flush all cached thumbnails and in-memory references.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Metavoid is a general audience utility tool. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Since no personal data or account creation exists, no children's data is retained.

5. Contact for Privacy Requests

For inquiries regarding this policy or technical verification, contact: contact@metavoid.app.

Cookie & Storage Policy

Last revised: September 2026

1. No Tracking or Marketing Cookies

Metavoid operates a cookie-free architecture. We do not set HTTP tracking cookies, marketing cookies, third-party analytics cookies, or cross-site identifiers.

2. Essential Browser Local Storage

The application only uses your browser's built-in localStorage API for strictly necessary interface functions:

  • metavoid_mode: Remembers your selected cleaning mode (e.g. "Remove Only AI Metadata" vs "Remove All Metadata").
  • metavoid_storage_ack: Remembers that you have dismissed the informational storage banner so it does not interrupt you again.

This information stays entirely inside your browser sandbox on your device and is never sent to our servers or any third parties.
