================================================================================
PRIVACY & ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY
================================================================================

1. 100% In-Memory Processing: Image metadata removal takes place strictly in
   volatile RAM. Files are never written to permanent disk storage by the engine.
2. Zero Training Retention: We never store your images, and we never use your
   images, metadata, or generation prompts to train, fine-tune, or evaluate
   any AI or machine learning models.
3. Offline First: When running locally, processing occurs strictly on your
   device (127.0.0.1). Zero bytes of image data leave your machine.
4. Zero Tracking: No HTTP tracking cookies, marketing trackers, or third-party
   analytics identifiers.
